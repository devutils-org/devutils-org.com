## A particular research result

Krizhevsky, Sutskever and Hinton's 2012 paper reports image classification using a deep convolutional network and an efficient GPU implementation. It documents a combination of data, training, architecture and evaluation rather than the invention of every neural network or the beginning of artificial intelligence.

## Keep the benchmark attached to its task

An image-classification result concerns the stated data and evaluation procedure. It cannot establish how a system will perform on every new population, prove general intelligence or supply a complete explanation of later AI systems.

## Context and limits

The [modern-computing chapter](/history/modern-computing/) places this publication beside the much earlier Dartmouth proposal, GPU programming, data evaluation and physical resource limits. Its classification-accuracy exercises are invented and must not be confused with this paper's measured results. This event uses publication-year precision. No model training, benchmark reproduction or independent replication was performed for the site's assessment.
