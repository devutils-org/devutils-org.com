## A different engineering question

Shannon’s paper treats the reproduction of a selected message at another point. Its introduction separates this engineering problem from the meaning of a particular message and discusses noise and the statistical structure of messages. The accessible Bell Labs record dates the publication to July 1948. The publisher DOI is retained below as a bibliographic reference; direct retrieval required a robot check during this source audit.

## Place it beside the circuit work

The earlier switching-circuit work concerns the behavior and design of networks. Communication theory asks a different set of questions about messages and channels. Read the linked profile and circuit event to compare the two connections without treating them as the same theory.

## Scope of this entry

This is a publication milestone, not a full explanation of entropy, coding or channel capacity. Those topics need their own worked examples and source review. The timeline uses year precision rather than assigning an exact date to the entire development of the theory.
