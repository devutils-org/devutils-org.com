## Contribution

Babbage designed calculating engines, including the Analytical Engine with instructions represented on cards. His projects connect mechanical engineering with the organization of calculation.

## Context and limits

Distinguish a design from a completed operational machine. Babbage did not finish an operational Analytical Engine during his lifetime.
