## Contribution

Wilson worked with Steve Furber on the BBC Micro and ARM architecture, and wrote the BBC BASIC interpreter.

## Context and limits

Instruction sets, hardware implementations and language tools are separate parts of a computer. This work connects all three through a collaborative design project.
