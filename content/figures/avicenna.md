## Contribution

Avicenna revised syllogistic reasoning, including the treatment of modal propositions and their logical relations.

## Context and limits

His work belongs to an active Arabic-language tradition of logical research. Necessity, possibility and time require distinctions beyond a simple true-or-false label.
