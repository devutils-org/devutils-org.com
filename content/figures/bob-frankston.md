## Contribution

Frankston founded Software Arts with Dan Bricklin to develop and sell the VisiCalc spreadsheet for personal computers.

## Context and limits

Turning a spreadsheet idea into usable software required implementation as well as interaction design. VisiCalc is a collaborative contribution.
