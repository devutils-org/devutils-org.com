## Contribution

Leibniz worked on mechanical calculation and binary arithmetic, alongside mathematics and philosophy.

## Context and limits

A binary representation describes numbers using two digits; it does not alone specify a computer architecture. His symbolic ambitions and the later reception of his work require separate historical study.
