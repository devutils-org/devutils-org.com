## Contribution

Noyce proposed a monolithic integrated-circuit structure using metal interconnections above an oxide layer, building on Jean Hoerni’s planar process.

## Context and limits

The account distinguishes his interconnection approach from Kilby’s earlier demonstration and identifies the Fairchild team that produced working circuits.
