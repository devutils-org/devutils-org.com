## Contribution

Dignāga developed accounts of inference and argument in Indian Buddhist philosophy, including the wheel of reasons.

## Context and limits

The tradition distinguishes inference as a cognitive activity from its verbal expression for others. Transmission and interpretation of surviving texts require care.
