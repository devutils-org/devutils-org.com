## Contribution

Backus led work on FORTRAN and contributed to notation for specifying programming-language syntax.

## Context and limits

A grammar describes valid structure; a compiler also has to translate meaning into executable operations. FORTRAN’s development was a team effort aimed at practical scientific computation.
