## Contribution

Gödel proved completeness for first-order logic and incompleteness results for formal arithmetic.

## Context and limits

The results concern different notions of completeness. Incompleteness requires hypotheses about the formal theory; it is not a statement that all axiomatic systems fail in the same way.
