## Contribution

Al-Khwarizmi wrote on Hindu-Arabic numerals and algebra. The word algorithm derives from the transmission of his name.

## Context and limits

That linguistic history does not mean he invented every kind of algorithm. His work is an entry into the transmission of written calculation methods across languages.
