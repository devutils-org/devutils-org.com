## Contribution

Berners-Lee developed the World Wide Web at CERN by combining hypertext with Internet communication and resource identifiers.

## Context and limits

The Web uses the Internet but is not identical to it. The history also includes Robert Cailliau, browser developers and the decision to make Web technology freely available.
