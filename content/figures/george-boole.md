## Contribution

Boole brought algebraic methods into the study of logic. His mathematical work also included probability and differential equations.

## Context and limits

Boolean algebra is useful for describing logical functions. Connecting it to physical switching circuits required later engineering and mathematical work, including Shannon’s.
