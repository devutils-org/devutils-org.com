## Contribution

Jackson worked in NACA’s West Area Computing section before moving into wind-tunnel research and aeronautical engineering.

## Context and limits

Her work was not the same as Katherine Johnson’s trajectory calculations or Dorothy Vaughan’s programming. Distinguishing their roles makes the shared institutional setting clearer.
