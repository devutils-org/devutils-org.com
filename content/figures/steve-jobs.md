## Contribution

Jobs co-founded Apple with Steve Wozniak and later founded NeXT, contributing leadership in personal computing and interface design.

## Context and limits

Product leadership differs from sole authorship of hardware or software. These systems depended on engineering and design teams as well as business decisions.
