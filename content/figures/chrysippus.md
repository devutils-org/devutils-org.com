## Contribution

Chrysippus developed Stoic reasoning about whole propositions joined by connectives. This differs from studying only relations between category terms.

## Context and limits

His writings survive indirectly. Modern truth tables can help explain distinctions, but should not be presented as his notation or as a complete reconstruction of Stoic conditionals.
