## Contribution

Berry collaborated with John Atanasoff to design and build the Atanasoff-Berry Computer at Iowa State.

## Context and limits

His role illustrates why an engineering partnership should be named explicitly. The system’s historical importance does not make it equivalent to every later computer architecture.
