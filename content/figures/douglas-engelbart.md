## Contribution

Engelbart and his laboratory developed interactive tools including the mouse and NLS, combining text, hypertext and collaboration.

## Context and limits

His goal was to augment human problem-solving. The lab’s work should be understood as a collaborative system project rather than the invention of one isolated input device.
