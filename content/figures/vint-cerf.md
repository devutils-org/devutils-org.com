## Contribution

Cerf co-developed the TCP/IP protocol suite with Robert Kahn and helped guide the development of internetworking.

## Context and limits

The Internet joins networks through common protocols. It is broader than the Web, which supplies a particular way to identify, link and exchange resources over it.
