## Contribution

The name Satoshi Nakamoto appears on the Bitcoin paper proposing peer-to-peer electronic cash with proof-of-work transaction ordering.

## Context and limits

The paper documents a protocol proposal, not a verified civil identity. Its security discussion depends on assumptions about participating and adversarial computing power.
