## Contribution

Euclid organized mathematical propositions and proofs in the Elements. The work became a durable model of systematic mathematical exposition.

## Context and limits

Separating assumptions from derived statements remains useful when reading a proof or a program specification. This is a teaching connection, rather than a claim that Euclid anticipated software.
