## Contribution

Kay contributed to object-oriented programming, personal computing and graphical interfaces, and led the team that developed Smalltalk.

## Context and limits

The work took place among colleagues at ARPA and Xerox PARC. Personal computing involved ideas about learning and interaction as well as processor hardware.
