## Contribution

Faggin developed silicon-gate technology and led implementation work on Intel’s MCS-4 chips, working with Masatoshi Shima.

## Context and limits

Microprocessor history includes circuit techniques, architecture and fabrication. The 4004 was a team project rather than one person’s isolated invention.
