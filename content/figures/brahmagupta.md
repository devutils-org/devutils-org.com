## Contribution

Brahmagupta wrote on mathematics and astronomy, including number systems, square-root procedures and quadratic equations.

## Context and limits

His mathematical setting belongs to the history of explicit calculation procedures. Modern algorithms should be compared with the actual procedures rather than attributed wholesale to a single ancient author.
