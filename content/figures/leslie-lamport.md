## Contribution

Lamport contributed to the theory and design of distributed and concurrent systems and created the initial LaTeX document preparation system.

## Context and limits

Reasoning about independent processes requires explicit assumptions about communication and ordering. Typesetting is a separate contribution, building on Knuth’s TeX.
