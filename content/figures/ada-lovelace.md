## Contribution

Lovelace translated an account of Babbage’s Analytical Engine and expanded it with notes explaining its programming possibilities.

## Context and limits

Her work developed through collaboration with Babbage. Describing that collaboration is more informative than treating a contested first-programmer label as a complete biography.
