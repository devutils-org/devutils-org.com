## Contribution

Hellman co-authored New Directions in Cryptography with Whitfield Diffie and contributed to public-key cryptography alongside Diffie and Ralph Merkle.

## Context and limits

The work changed how key distribution could be approached over insecure communication channels. Credit belongs to a research context as well as the names attached to a protocol.
