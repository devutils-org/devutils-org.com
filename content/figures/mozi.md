## Contribution

Mozi’s teachings initiated the Mohist tradition of explicit argument, standards of judgment and debate in ancient China.

## Context and limits

Later Mohist work developed more elaborate accounts of language and reasoning. These should not all be assigned to Mozi personally or treated as a direct ancestry of electronic computers.
