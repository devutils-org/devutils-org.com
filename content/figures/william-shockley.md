## Contribution

Shockley organized Bell Labs’ solid-state group and pursued semiconductor amplifier concepts. Bardeen and Brattain achieved the point-contact device within that research setting.

## Context and limits

Distinguishing leadership, proposals and experimental realization avoids assigning the same contribution to all members of a team.
