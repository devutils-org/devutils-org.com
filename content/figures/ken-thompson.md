## Contribution

Thompson co-created Unix with Dennis Ritchie at Bell Labs and worked on languages and operating systems including B and Plan 9.

## Context and limits

Unix connected a compact operating-system design with reusable programming tools. Its later descendants reflect contributions from many communities.
