## Contribution

McIlroy developed the idea of Unix pipes and promoted composing programs from small tools that exchange streams.

## Context and limits

Connecting tools by a shared interface changes how software can be assembled. The proposal and its implementation within Unix involved different members of the Bell Labs team.
